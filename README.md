# Nystagmus Vision Simulator

**Nystagmus Vision Simulator** is an interactive, browser-based educational tool that helps people better understand what living with congenital nystagmus can be like.

Unlike simple blur effects, this simulator models continuous involuntary eye movement using two independently animated eye views displayed through realistic eyeglass lenses. Each lens can move independently or together, reflecting how eye movements may vary between the left and right eye. The simulator also recreates brief **null point** moments—short periods when eye movement temporarily decreases and vision becomes clearer—providing a more authentic representation of the condition.

Designed for education, awareness, demonstrations, and conversations with family, friends, teachers, employers, and healthcare professionals, the simulator allows users to upload any image and adjust numerous parameters in real time to explore different visual experiences.

## Features

* 👓 Realistic dual-eye eyeglass simulation
* 👁️ Independent left and right eye movement
* 🔄 Multiple movement patterns (horizontal, vertical, diagonal, circular, and irregular)
* ⚡ Adjustable movement speed from slow to extremely rapid
* 📈 Adjustable movement severity
* 🌫️ Dynamic motion blur based on eye movement
* 🎯 Configurable **Null Point** simulation with adjustable frequency and strength
* 🌊 Drift and jerkiness controls for more natural eye motion
* 🖼️ Upload any image to instantly simulate how it may appear
* 🌐 Runs entirely in the browser using HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript
* 💻 No installation, server, or external dependencies required

## Purpose

This project is **not intended as a medical diagnostic tool**. Every person with congenital nystagmus experiences vision differently, and no simulation can perfectly reproduce an individual's perception.

Its purpose is to improve awareness and understanding by providing a realistic, interactive demonstration of how involuntary eye movement, motion blur, and brief moments of increased clarity can affect everyday vision.
